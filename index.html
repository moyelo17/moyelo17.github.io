Daily market show

Today's close. Tomorrow's open. Explained.

Next Open is a short, no-hype show on what moved the US stock market today and what to watch at the next open: the S&P 500, Nasdaq and Dow, the price levels traders are watching, Federal Reserve and economic data, earnings, and insider trades.

Every story is built from primary sources - SEC filings, the Federal Reserve, the U.S. Treasury and official economic releases - and every source is listed under each episode.

Watch on YouTube

Schedule

A new episode every US trading day, published in the evening after the market close (Sunday to Thursday evenings, US time).

How the show is made

Next Open is produced with the help of software tools, including AI-generated voices. Each episode is checked against its sources before it is published, and videos are labeled on YouTube as containing altered or synthetic content where required.

Next Open Publisher

"Next Open Publisher" is the channel's own internal tool. It uses the YouTube API, with the channel owner's permission, to upload Next Open episodes to the Next Open channel and to read that channel's own analytics so the show can improve. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's data. See the privacy policy and terms.

Not financial advice

Everything published by Next Open is general market commentary for education and entertainment. It is not financial, investment or trading advice, and nothing is a recommendation to buy or sell any security. Do your own research and consult a licensed professional before investing.
